7 × 7 GAME CONCEPT / SKILL DECK V1

24 skills. No two alike.

Nature and object illustrations inspired by the project's parchment and ink cards. Each skill is single-use. Mana values are initial playtest proposals.

Editable card definitions · Rules and integration notes · Generation prompts

01 · SIDESTEP

1 MANA · MOVEMENT

Move one friendly piece 1 square horizontally or vertically to an empty square.

02 · DASH

2 MANA · MOVEMENT

Move one friendly piece exactly 2 squares horizontally or vertically. Both squares must be empty.

03 · BLINK

3 MANA · MOVEMENT

Teleport one friendly piece to an empty square within distance 3. Ignore pieces between.

04 · EXCHANGE

2 MANA · MOVEMENT

Swap two friendly pieces within distance 3 of each other.

05 · ADVANCE

3 MANA · MOVEMENT

Move two different friendly pieces 1 square each toward the enemy starting edge. Both destinations must be empty.

06 · RECALL

1 MANA · MOVEMENT

Teleport one friendly piece to any empty square on your starting row.

07 · STRIKE

3 MANA · ATTACK

Choose a friendly piece. Eliminate 1 enemy horizontally or vertically adjacent to it. Your piece stays still.

08 · PIERCE

4 MANA · ATTACK

Choose a friendly piece. Eliminate an enemy exactly 2 squares away horizontally or vertically. The square between must be empty.

09 · SURROUND

2 MANA · ATTACK

Eliminate 1 enemy horizontally or vertically adjacent to at least two friendly pieces.

10 · SWEEP

5 MANA · ATTACK

Choose a friendly piece. Eliminate up to 2 enemies horizontally or vertically adjacent to it.

11 · SHOVE

1 MANA · CONTROL

Choose a friendly piece. Push an adjacent enemy 1 square directly away. The destination must be empty and on the board.

12 · PULL

1 MANA · CONTROL

Choose a friendly piece. Pull an enemy exactly 2 squares away horizontally or vertically into the empty square between.

13 · VAULT

2 MANA · MOVEMENT

Move one friendly piece exactly 2 squares horizontally or vertically to an empty square. You may jump over a piece.

14 · FLANK

3 MANA · ATTACK

Move one friendly piece 1 square diagonally. The destination must be empty or contain an enemy. Eliminate that enemy if present.

15 · TRANSPOSITION

4 MANA · CONTROL

Swap one friendly piece with one enemy within distance 2. Neither piece is eliminated.

16 · GUST

3 MANA · CONTROL

Move up to 2 enemies 1 square each in the same horizontal or vertical direction. Both destinations must be empty before the skill.

17 · RALLY

3 MANA · MOVEMENT

Choose a friendly anchor. Move up to 2 other friendly pieces 1 square each horizontally or vertically toward it. Each move must reduce distance and land empty.

18 · SCATTER

3 MANA · MOVEMENT

Move up to 2 different friendly pieces 1 square diagonally each. Both destinations must be empty before the skill.

19 · CROSSFIRE

4 MANA · ATTACK

Eliminate an enemy between two friendly pieces in one row or column. Each ally must be within distance 3 with no pieces between it and the target.

20 · CHAIN STRIKE

5 MANA · ATTACK

Choose a friendly piece. Eliminate an adjacent enemy, then optionally 1 enemy adjacent to the first target. Adjacency is horizontal or vertical.

21 · MANA SURGE

0 MANA · RESOURCE

Gain 2 mana, up to the mana cap. This uses your skill for the turn.

22 · REFOCUS

1 MANA · RESOURCE

Return both movement cards to the movement deck. Shuffle, then draw 2 movement cards.

23 · FORESIGHT

1 MANA · RESOURCE

Look at the top 3 skill cards, or all remaining cards if fewer. Return them to the top in any order before drawing your replacement.

24 · BANISH

4 MANA · CONTROL

Choose a friendly piece. Send an adjacent enemy to an empty square on that enemy's starting row. Adjacency is horizontal or vertical.
