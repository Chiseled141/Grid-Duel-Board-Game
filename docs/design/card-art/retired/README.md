# Retired card artwork

Full-size sources moved here from `src/main/resources/assets/cards/manuscript-v1/` when the
neutral card set replaced them (catalog `cards-neutral-1`). They are kept for reference only:
nothing in the game loads them, they are outside the built jar, and their runtime copies in
`assets/cards/runtime/` were deleted.

- **National Landmarks and Spells** (`*-banner-hall`, `hu-stud-farm`, `pl-royal-court`,
  `bo-wagon-fort`, `au-chancery`, `st-armoury`, `cr-cathedral`, `tr-fortress-church`, and the
  14 national Spells): replaced by the neutral Spells and Landmarks in
  `../drafts/neutral-v4/`. Spells and Landmarks no longer have a nationality, and Destroy
  Landmark was removed from the rules.
- **Chaplain and Envoy** Units for all seven nations: retired when the five Unit classes were
  finalized (Footmen, Pikemen, Bowmen, Riders, Knights).

`../gallery.html`, `../manifest.json`, `../status/` and `../title-check.json` remain the record of
the original manuscript art pass; the gallery's links to these images point here.
