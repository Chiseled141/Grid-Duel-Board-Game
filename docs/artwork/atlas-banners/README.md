# Painted atlas banner concepts

Two concepts completed with the built-in image generation tool before the user
interrupted generation to request a branch and commit. They are not connected
to the running Atlas. Poland/Austria generation was interrupted, and no completed
outputs were available when this snapshot was saved. The runtime still uses the
sourced flag artwork in assets/atlas/flags.

References: the corresponding downloaded historical banner and the game's
assets/battlefield.png menu illustration. The Hungary result was also intended
as a style reference for subsequent banners.

- hungary-painted-v1.png: generated interpretation of Thommy's CC0 Vladislaus I
  banner, with Hungarian stripes and white eagles.
- bohemia-painted-v1.png: generated adaptation of Dragovit's CC BY-SA 4.0 Bohemian
  royal banner. This adaptation remains under CC BY-SA 4.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Royal_Banner_of_the_Kingdom_of_Bohemia.png

These are artistic concepts, not authenticated historical flags. The Bohemian
concept's background/transparency needs checking before runtime integration.

Prompt used for both, with the faction-specific instructions below:

Use case: style-transfer. Deliver ONE isolated medieval cloth flag asset for a
historical atlas UI, genuinely TRANSPARENT background. Image 1 is heraldic design
reference, image 2 is the game's approved menu art STYLE reference. Reinterpret
image 1 as an actual loosely hand-painted cloth banner that belongs to image 2:
warm afternoon illumination, subdued crimson, buttery ivory, visible gouache
brush shapes, soft natural shading, gently aged linen, a few subtle frayed
threads. Graceful mild wind ripples but mostly front-facing so the full emblem
remains legible at thumbnail size. Whole banner fully inside canvas with generous
margin. Simple short dark wooden hoist at left, no metal ornament. Painterly 2D
animation-background style, no photorealistic fabric, no shiny 3D render, no
vector-flat clipart, no black comic contours, no ornate gold border, no UI, no
lettering, no scenery. Preserve exact overall heraldic layout and colors of
image 1; do not invent extra symbols. Strong readable silhouette, fabric folds
do not hide emblems. Wide landscape canvas.

Hungary: preserve the reference's quartered red/ivory Hungarian stripes and white
Polish eagles, including its elongated trailing pennant form. Render the white
stripes and eagles as opaque ivory pigment, not transparency.

Bohemia: crimson banner with ONE crowned silver/ivory rampant double-tailed lion
facing the hoist, gold crown and claws, following the reference closely. Preserve
the distinctive two tails; do not turn this into a realistic animal or an eagle.
Opaque ivory lion on crimson cloth.
