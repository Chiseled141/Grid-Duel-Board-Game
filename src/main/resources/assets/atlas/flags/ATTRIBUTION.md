## Historical identification

- Hungary: Vladislaus I's royal banner, 1440–1444. Commons cites Győző Somogyi,
  *Magyar hadizászlók* (2011), p.25, and Zoltán Horváth (1995), p.14.
- Poland: the source describes the medieval White Eagle banner established in
  the 14th century. This specific reconstruction is not authenticated to 1444.
- Bohemia: historical heraldic royal-banner reconstruction; no exact-1444 claim.
- Austria: Babenberg/Austrian red-white-red heraldic banner reconstruction.
  Medieval arms attested from 1230; reference:
  https://www.aeiou.at/aeiou.encyclop.b/b486228.htm;internal&action=_setlanguage.action?LANGUAGE=en
- Styria: Jimmy44 cites the medieval Gelre Armorial, folio 70v; a ducal heraldic
  banner, not today's white-green state flag.
- Croatia: Talovac campaign/family banner, associated by the source with Varna;
  Colin Imber, *The Crusade of Varna, 1443–45*, p.82. Not presented as a single
  standardized national flag for all Croatian lands.
- Transylvania: no distinct flag of the voivodeship in 1444 has been verified
  here. The Hungarian king's banner is a crown reference ONLY. We deliberately
  do not substitute a later princely banner or the Hunyadi extended-arms flag
  (whose adapted arms date to 1453). Background:
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voivode_of_Transylvania

The AI-generated reference map still has its original baked-in heraldry. This
change replaces the inspector's extracted crest with sourced banner artwork;
it does not claim to correct all symbols embedded in that reference map.
