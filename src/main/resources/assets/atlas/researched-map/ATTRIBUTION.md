Chalstrey, Benam, Cioni, Reddish, Zsambok, Levine, Cook, Hoyer and Turchin (2025).

Adaptations: 1440–1449 record selection, regional reconstruction, equal-area projection,
coast clipping, disjoint pixel masks and SVG contour tracing. Regional internal boundaries
are approximate reconstructions, not independently verified 1444 survey data.

Physical reference: Natural Earth shaded relief and land geometry (public domain):
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Terrain repainted using the built-in image generation tool. Fine terrain details are illustrative.
